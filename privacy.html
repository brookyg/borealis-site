Privacy Notice

Last updated: [ADD DATE]

This notice explains how Borealis Design ("we", "us") handles your personal information when you contact us through this website. We take your privacy seriously and only collect what we need to help with your enquiry.

What we collect

When you complete our enquiry form, we collect the details you give us — typically your name, email address, and the information you share about your project.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • Where relevant, and only with your consent, to introduce you to a vetted local trade who can carry out the work.

Sharing your details

We will only pass your details to a trusted local contractor where you have agreed to this by ticking the consent box on our form, and only for the purpose of progressing your project. We do not sell your data, and we never share it with anyone for marketing unrelated to your enquiry.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry details only as long as needed to help with your project and our legitimate business records, after which they are securely deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access the information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To do any of these, email us at hello@borealisdesign.co.uk.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your data? Email hello@borealisdesign.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
