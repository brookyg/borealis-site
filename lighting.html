The idea

One ceiling light is not
a lighting scheme.

Great lighting works in three layers. Get the balance right and an ordinary room feels considered, warm and expensive. That layering — and choosing fixtures that belong together — is what we do.

Ambient

The base layer — soft, even light that sets the overall mood of the room without glare.

Task

Focused light where you actually do things — worktops, reading nooks, the mirror, the desk.

Accent

The drama — picking out a texture, a piece of art, an alcove. The detail people feel but rarely notice.
